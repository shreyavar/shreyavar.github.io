Shreya Vardhan

I am a theoretical physicist working at the intersection of quantum information, quantum gravity, and quantum many-body physics.

I am currently a postdoctoral scholar at the Institute for Quantum Information and Matter at Caltech. I was a postdoc at the Stanford Institute for Theoretical Physics from 2022 to 2025. I received my PhD at MIT in 2022, working with Professor Hong Liu.
